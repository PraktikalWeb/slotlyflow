# Product Vision

## Product

**SlotlyFlow**

## Purpose

SlotlyFlow enables businesses to automate customer communication on WhatsApp while retaining seamless human consultant takeover.

The platform must hide technical Meta/WhatsApp infrastructure from ordinary business users.

## Primary customer outcome

A business owner should be able to:

1. Create an account.
2. Create a business workspace.
3. Connect an eligible WhatsApp Business number.
4. Configure business information and an automation.
5. Configure operating hours and human handover.
6. Test the setup.
7. Activate it.
8. Manage customer conversations from SlotlyFlow.
9. Review basic operational analytics.

## Product principles

- Non-technical first.
- Secure by default.
- Human handover is a first-class capability.
- Automation behavior must be predictable.
- Business owners should not need to understand WABA IDs, phone-number IDs, access tokens, webhook tokens, app IDs, or infrastructure.
- Core messaging must remain useful without AI.
- AI may enhance the platform later but must not become a required dependency for deterministic workflows.
- The platform should remain portable across hosting providers.

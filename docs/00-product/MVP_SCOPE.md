# MVP Scope

## In scope

### Account and workspace
- Email/password account registration.
- Email verification.
- Login/logout.
- Password recovery.
- Organization/business workspace.
- Organization membership.
- Roles: OWNER, ADMIN, AGENT.

### WhatsApp
- Connect a supported WhatsApp Business account through the approved Meta onboarding mechanism.
- Persist connection metadata securely.
- Receive webhooks.
- Send and receive supported WhatsApp messages.
- Track message states.
- Surface connection health in user-friendly language.

### Contacts and conversations
- Contact record.
- Conversation record.
- Message history.
- Unread state.
- Realtime inbox updates.
- Agent reply.

### Automation
- Deterministic flow execution.
- Versioned flow definitions.
- Triggers.
- Text messages.
- Menus/buttons/list-like choices where provider rules permit.
- Input collection.
- Branching.
- Variables.
- Operating-hours conditions.
- Fallback behavior.
- Timeout behavior.
- End flow.
- Template-based starting configurations.

### Human handover
- Request handover.
- Waiting state.
- Agent assignment/takeover.
- Pause automation.
- Resume automation according to explicit policy.
- Audit handover events.

### Business knowledge
- Basic business profile.
- Services.
- Prices where supplied.
- FAQs.
- Operating hours.
- Contact/location information.
- Policies/links.

### Platform operations
- Basic analytics.
- Usage metering.
- Subscription/billing foundation.
- Super-admin operational visibility.
- Audit logs.
- Observability.

## Explicitly out of MVP

- Full CRM replacement.
- Visual Zapier-style general workflow engine.
- Omnichannel inbox.
- Instagram automation.
- Facebook Messenger automation.
- SMS automation.
- Native iOS/Android applications.
- Voice/calling.
- Advanced marketing campaign suite.
- General integrations marketplace.
- Full sales pipeline.
- Autonomous AI agents that can independently change business state.
- Arbitrary customer-authored code execution.
